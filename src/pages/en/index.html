    Have questions?

    How Long Will the Journey Last?

    The Ecolution Challenge begins on February 1 and runs through March 2. For those ready to dive even deeper, we offer a 42-day marathon that extends until March 14. This extended program provides you with even more insights, resources, and opportunities for personal growth. At the end of your journey, recipients of our challenge will be honored with a symbolic medal and a certificate, recognizing your commitment to positive change.

    What is the Cost of Participation?

    Participation is absolutely free! All we ask is that you bring your enthusiasm, a willingness to learn, and a commitment to embracing a more ethical lifestyle. Ecolution seeks to make veganism accessible to everyone—no matter your background or financial situation. We believe that good food should be accessible to all.

    What Will I Experience?
    • Nutritional Mastery: Gain insights into essential nutrients and how to design meals that nourish your body.
    • Creative Meal Planning: Discover strategies to effortlessly plan your meals and streamline your grocery shopping.
    • Culinary Skills: Participate in interactive cooking demonstrations that will empower you to prepare vibrant and delicious plant-based meals.
    • Mentorship: Connect with experienced volunteers who will guide you every step of the way, answering your questions and providing motivation.
    • Online Resources: Access a treasure trove of recipes, articles, and videos designed to make your transition seamless and enjoyable.
    • Community Connection: Participate in forums where you can share triumphs, seek advice, and inspire one another throughout the challenge.
    What Does “Veganism” Mean?

    During the challenge, participants will try to live a vegan lifestyle, which means excluding all kinds of meat, dairy products, eggs, and honey from the diet; and refusing to use any products obtained through the exploitation of animals. If you’re wondering why the above products are not considered vegan and how to uphold a moral‑ethical minimum in everyday life, we will answer these questions in detail during the challenge. In the meantime, you can visit Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veganism) or talk to an artificial intelligence.
